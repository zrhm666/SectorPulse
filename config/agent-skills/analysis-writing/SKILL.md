# Analysis writing

Use this method to turn completed sector research into a clear article and to revise specific passages after review.

1. Name the exact sector subject in every section heading and opening sentence. Keep similarly named companies, products, concepts, and industries distinct.
2. Build the article around one restrained thesis. Order sections by explanatory value, then make the transition between sections explicit instead of presenting an unrelated list.
3. Separate observed market facts, reported news facts, interpretation, and unresolved uncertainty. Use wording whose confidence matches the underlying research.
4. Write titles that identify the market phenomenon without promising a cause or outcome. Use the introduction to state what changed and how the article will examine it.
5. Within each section, lead with the subject and observation, then explain relevant evidence, counterpoints, and limitations. Do not turn temporal proximity or thematic similarity into causal certainty.
6. End with a synthesis that preserves differences between sectors. Keep the risk notice concise and separate from the analytical conclusion.
7. When revising, address each cited concern directly. Preserve sound passages, avoid unrelated rewrites, and check that changed wording remains consistent with the rest of the article.
